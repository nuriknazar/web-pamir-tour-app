How it works

Booking your Pamir adventure is simple.

We take care of the important details so you can focus on enjoying the journey.

  1. Choose Your Tour: Explore our Pamir tours and choose the itinerary that best fits your interests, schedule, and travel style. Whether you want to discover the Wakhan Valley, explore the Pamir Highway, visit Murghab, or experience local villages and culture, we have options for you.
  2. Send Us Your Details: Select your preferred tour dates, number of travelers, and any special requests. Submit the booking request through our website, and our team will get back to you to confirm availability and answer any questions.
  3. Confirm Your Booking: Once your tour is confirmed, we will provide the final itinerary, price, payment instructions, and information about any required permits or documents. We can also help you with practical travel arrangements before your arrival in Tajikistan.
  4. Get Ready for the Pamirs: Before your trip, we'll help you prepare with information about transportation, accommodation, packing, permits, weather, and what to expect along the route.
  5. Meet Your Guide & Start Your Adventure: On the agreed date, your driver and/or guide will meet you at the arranged location. From there, your Pamir adventure begins—through dramatic mountain landscapes, remote valleys, high-altitude passes, and welcoming local communities.
  6. Enjoy the Journey: Travel at a comfortable pace, discover places beyond the usual tourist routes, and experience the Pamirs with a local team that knows the region.

Ready to explore the Pamirs? Choose your tour and send us your booking request. We'll take care of the rest.

Travel documents

Apply online with your Tajikistan e-Visa

  • Go through the official Tajikistan e-Visa system: Tajikistan e-Visa
  • During the application, select the option for the GBAO/special border-area permit. Tajikistan's official guidance says the permit is attached to the electronic visa.
  • Make sure the GBAO permit is actually shown on your approved visa before traveling.

Confirm your ride

GBAO permit required

Every foreign traveler needs a GBAO permit to enter the Tajik Pamir, separate from your Tajikistan visa. It can't be obtained at the border — apply in advance via evisa.tj or at an OVIR office. Checkpoints along the highway will check for it.
